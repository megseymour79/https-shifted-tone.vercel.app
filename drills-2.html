Practice the reset.

These drills help you create space before a conversation gets tense. Keep the words simple and the pace steady.

04

Name the pause

Say, “I want to answer that carefully.” Take one breath before continuing.

05

Make the request clear

State what you need in one sentence. Remove apologies and extra explanations.

06

Close with certainty

Repeat the next step in a calm, complete sentence so everyone knows what happens next.
