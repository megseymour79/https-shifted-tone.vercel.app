Who is ShiftedTone for?

Leaders, operators, sales professionals, and anyone who wants to sound as calm and clear as they intend.

Yes. The drills are designed for everyday conversations and can be repeated whenever you need them.

Is it private?

Yes. The site does not ask you to publish personal identity data.

It means the site is ready to use as a simple static practice guide.
