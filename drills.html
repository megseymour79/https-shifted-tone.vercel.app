Start with one skill.

Choose one drill. Speak out loud for about a minute, then repeat it once with the adjustment in mind.

01

Slow the first sentence

Say your opening sentence at half your usual speed. Leave a small pause before the next thought.

02

Land the last word

Read a short message and finish each sentence without trailing off. Keep the volume even.

03

Replace the reflex

Say, “Let me think about that.” Pause, breathe once, and then answer in one clear sentence.
